📖 GPO Trading Rules & Guide

Official trading economy rules from the Grand Piece Online community document. How not to get scammed and what to watch out for.

1. Hierarchy & Ranking Order

An item's position within its tier strictly determines its value! Items placed higher in a tier are always worth more than those below. Being in the same tier does NOT guarantee equal value.

💡 Example: All Seeing Eye (ASE) is ranked higher than Troll Face and World Ender in the same A+ tier.

2. The Unstable Items Trap

Items in the Very Unstable category (e.g. Valkyrie Helmets, Gift of Fruit, Clown Wig) do not have fixed values. Their price fluctuates heavily based on hype and individual buyers.

⚠️ Be careful: Do not trade stable liquid fruits (Mochi, Venom, Pika) for unstable items!

3. Trading Currency: Chests (LC & MC)

Legendary Fruit Chest (LC) is the universally accepted standard currency in the game. Most traders express values in number of LC chests. A Mythical Chest (MC) is usually valued at 10-12 LC.

4. Demand Over Nominal Value

An item with High Demand can easily be traded for an overpay (+20% value). Items with Low Demand often have to be traded at a discount.
