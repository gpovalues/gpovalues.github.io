Read the market, not just a number

How the catalogue and estimates work.

The official order

Regular tiers keep their order from the official document. New and Very Unstable items can move by estimated value. A new document can change the official order.

Open the official tier list

One consistent scale

SP Reset is the reference: 1 point. An item’s LC equivalent is its value divided by the current value of one Legendary Chest. A pack of ten always equals ten single chests.

Offers are not sales

A barter relationship needs at least five independent traders before it can affect prices. Quantities may differ between offers. Repeated posts from one author do not gain extra influence. Incomplete offers, unknown items and unspecified adds are excluded from pricing.

Evidence stays visible

Open an item to see traders, freshness, asking-price range, point reference and history. Without a supported chain to SP Reset, the point scale remains historical and confidence is limited. With no recent evidence, the previous or preliminary value is retained. Unpriced items remain unpriced.

How prices are fitted

Whole barter bundles are fitted together with a robust loss that limits outliers. Newer offers have more weight. Chest ratios, the SP anchor and regular-tier order are checked together, including after rounding.

Scheduled collection

GitHub Actions collects on a schedule and resumes from its saved checkpoint. Missed history is backfilled across runs. A source failure pauses market repricing. Empty time windows stay empty.

The initial references are inherited estimates, not verified trades. Offer estimates do not guarantee that someone will accept your trade.
